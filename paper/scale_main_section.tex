Three frozen official Q4\_K\_M checkpoints cover Qwen2.5-1.5B,
SmolLM2-1.7B and Qwen2.5-7B Instruct (1.54/1.71/7.61B parameters)
\citep{qwen15gguf,smol17gguf,qwen7gguf}. A locally fixed, non-preregistered
protocol uses 256/64/128 disjoint Bayesian training/validation/test contexts.
Each test context has two unsupported stances and four conditions: base,
answer permutation, evidence paraphrase and informative new evidence.
Ten answer letters encode five probability reports and two literal styles.
We archive pinned weight hashes, native logits, candidate mass and prompt
fingerprints. No model weights are trained.

A common frozen Qwen-1.5B auditor sees evidence without stance, in two
complementary answer orders. Only two monotone calibration parameters are
fit on training cases, with regularization selected on validation cases.
Targets use the estimated posterior and $T_E=.03$, without test labels.
Fourteen methods include constant-training-mean, raw-auditor, audit-only,
nearest-report, pooled-stance and explicit oracle controls; all protocol
repair/soft-score settings are reported. Later controls are exploratory.
There are 4,224 unique primary requests. An exploratory extension adds 448
requests on 32 length-filtered GSM8K-derived numeric-claim correctness
questions \citep{cobbe2021}, transferring the calibrator unchanged. This
is not standard GSM8K solve accuracy.

Full calibrated Bayesian regret (.0829) scarcely differs from the constant
target (.0840); their difference has auditor-refit interval
$[-.0042,.0117]$. The external calibrated target (.2785) is worse in point
estimate than constant (.2626), with a wide difference interval
$[-.0196,.1216]$. Gains over base do not establish better evidence judgment
than shrinkage. Equal full risks across backbones are an identity, not
independent replications. Against informative evidence, full repair's mean
absolute update is .0087 versus the posterior's .2931, with only 54.7\%
correct update directions. On Qwen-7B paraphrases, soft-score-8 beats half
repair in both risk and KL. All 5,000 paired context-bootstrap comparisons,
500 auditor-refit resamples and adverse conditions are archived.

Format sensitivity remains material: Qwen-7B external base regret changes
from .4901 to .3219 after option permutation. SmolLM2's external mean
candidate mass is .5992 and its valid greedy-letter rate is 26.6\%; no cases
are excluded. These are conditional bank scores, not free-text rankings.
The larger study supports the audit-quality limitation, rather than a
claim of broadly effective epistemic correction.
